# Unverified catalog entries

Parts with `"verified": false` are listed here. A `null` spec means the value
could not be confirmed and was left blank rather than guessed.

## Phase 1 starter catalog

Every part in the Phase 1 starter catalog is a placeholder entry pending the
Phase 2 research pass. Port counts follow the published product names, but
power, weight and most depths are deliberately `null` until checked against
the manufacturer's spec sheet. The generic passive items (patch panels,
blanks, shelves, PDUs) use typical dimensions and will stay `verified: false`
as they don't correspond to one specific product.
