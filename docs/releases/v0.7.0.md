## What's new

- **Cables follow your cable management.** When the rack has cable managers or brush panels, cables drop into the nearest one, run along it, and go up or down the side of the rack when the two ends use different managers. Cables sharing a run are drawn as a bundle. A front-to-rear cable is drawn to the brush panel it passes through. The suggested cable length now measures along that route. Racks without managers look the same as before.
- **Two-finger panning on tablets.** Drag with two fingers to move around the rack when you're zoomed in. It never picks up a device by accident.
- **Power figures for mini PCs.** The Lenovo ThinkCentre M720q and M920q Tiny, Dell OptiPlex 7060 Micro and HP EliteDesk 800 G4 Mini now have typical power draw from the manufacturers' own data, so the templates no longer say "no power data". Their max is the power adapter's rating.
