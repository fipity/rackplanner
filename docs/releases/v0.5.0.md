## What's new

- **Templates.** New design → **Template** starts you from a 10″ mini rack, an nbn + UniFi starter in a DeskPi RackMate T1, a 12U 19″ homelab or a 9U network cabinet, instead of an empty rack.
- **Read-only share links.** **Share** in the editor makes a link anyone can open to see both views, the stats and the parts list, and download a PDF or JSON copy. They can't change anything, and they don't need your password if you use basic auth. **Stop sharing** turns the link off.
- **Heat output.** Stats shows the rack's heat in BTU/h, worked out from the power draw.
- **Airflow.** Servers and UPSs are assumed to breathe front to back, and you can set any device's airflow in the inspector. If one device blows hot air out of the rack face where another draws cool air in, you get a warning. Gear mounted on the rear rails counts as blowing the other way.
- **DeskPi RackMate rails** now show round tapped #10-32 holes, like the real racks, instead of square cage-nut holes.
- **More checked specs.** The Cisco CBS350 switches, Netgear GS728TP / GS752TP / GS752TPP and APC SMT1500 UPSs are now checked against the makers' datasheets. The CBS350-24P-4G gained its power figure, two Netgear weights and one depth were corrected, and the SMT2200RM2U's capacity is now 1920 W.
