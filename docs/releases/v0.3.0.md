## What's new

- **More nbn connection boxes.** The FTTP box (Nokia XS-010X-Q), the FTTC box (NetComm NDD-0300) and the indoor Fixed Wireless box join the HFC box, ready to sit on a shelf and take cables.
- **Coax.** A coax cable type, plus 12-port (19″) and 6-port (10″) F-type coax patch panels, so the antenna or HFC lead can land on a panel and patch across to the box.
- **Gear turns itself to fit.** Drop a box on a shelf it only fits lying down and it's laid on its side for you (and stood back up when there's room). The Shelves view shows a turned box by its side panel.
