## What's new

- **A wall beside the rack.** Turn it on with the wall button in the toolbar, then drag nbn boxes, modems and other desktop gear onto it, or pick "On the wall" in the inspector. It sits left of the rack in the front view and right of it in the rear view, and cables run between the wall and the rack like anywhere else.
- **Wall plates and outlets.** Data (1 or 2 × RJ45), TV/coax (F-type), phone (RJ11/RJ12), fibre (SC/APC) and a double power point, so you can draw the whole path from the wall to the router.
- **Phone lines.** A phone (RJ11) port and cable type. The FTTC box's WALL SOCKET is now a real port.
- **Proper fibre connector on the FTTP box.** It's a single green SC/APC port now, not SC duplex. Designs with a cable on the old port are updated when they open.
- **Easier to tell the nbn boxes apart.** The FTTP, FTTC and Fixed Wireless boxes have a small label on their front edge, and the FTTC box's GATEWAY jack is yellow, like the real one.
- **Screen readers** announce devices by name ("nbn HFC NTD (Arris CM8200B), on its side") instead of an internal ID.
