## What's new

- **Reverse parts so they face the rear.** Turn any part round so its faceplate points at the back of the rack: a switch with its ports at the back, or a patch panel facing the rear. Select it and press **R**, use **Reverse (face rear)** in its details, or right-click it. The rear view then shows its ports and the front view its back panel (a plain generated one when the catalog has no rear layout). It keeps the same U, depth and position, and its airflow reverses. A **↺ Rear-facing** badge marks it in both views and in exports. *Mounting* (which rails a part sits on) and *Facing* (which way it points) are now separate. Reversing a shelf turns everything on it round, and a 3D-printed mount turns with its device. Turning gear on its side has moved from R to **T**.
- **Select several parts.** Shift- or Ctrl-click to add parts to the selection; R and Delete then act on all of them as one undo step. Right-click a part for a menu (Reverse, Turn on its side, Duplicate, Remove).
- **Free panning and zooming.** The rack canvas pans and zooms like a map: drag empty space, Space + drag, middle or right drag, arrow keys, one or two fingers. The mouse wheel zooms towards the cursor, and trackpad scrolling pans; a **Scroll wheel** button in the canvas corner sets this if it guesses wrong for your mouse. Zoom from 10% to 800% (click the % to type a value), F to fit, 0 for 100%, Shift+2 to zoom to the selection. There's an optional minimap, a **Back to rack** button, and each design remembers its view. Press **?** for all the shortcuts.
- **Work from any view.** Gear on the other side of the rack (seen through it) can now be selected and moved from the rear view, and keeps its rails when you do. **Swap view** jumps between front and rear with the selected part kept in the middle, and **Show through** hides or shows the gear on the other side.
- **Edit in the Shelves tab.** Click a device in the shelf plans to select it, drag it along its shelf or onto another shelf (a dashed outline shows where it lands), or click a tray to select the shelf. A bar and arrow mark which way each device faces. The plans pan and zoom with the same controls as the rack, and remember their view.
- **Warnings and data.** A new **Closed back** rack option warns when a reversed part's ports would face a solid back panel or a wall. Side-to-side airflow in a front-to-back rack gets a note. Rack NAS, NVRs and common fanless switches now have an airflow in the catalog (best guesses are marked unconfirmed). The CSV, PDF and printable parts lists have a **Facing** column, and JSON exports keep each part's facing. Designs from earlier versions are upgraded automatically and look the same as before.
