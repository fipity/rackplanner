## What's new

- **3D-printed parts from MakerWorld links.** Click **Add 3D-printed part** and paste a MakerWorld model link. RackPlanner reads the model page once for the title, designer, licence, print profile and picture, then guesses the rack width, U height, part type and the device it holds ("UniFi NVR Instant" → the UniFi NVR Instant). You check everything in a form before saving. MakerWorld often blocks automated requests; when it does, the name comes from the link, you fill in the rest (and can paste or upload a picture), and **Refresh details** tries again later. Print files are never downloaded.
- **Mounts that hold devices.** Placing a printed mount puts its device inside it, with the device's real front panel showing through the cutout. Dragging the mount moves the device too, and removing it asks whether to keep the device. The stats warn when a device is too wide, tall or deep for its mount or the rack.
- **Drawn like a printed part.** Printed parts are drawn matte in your filament colour, with printed-on rack ears, faint layer lines up close and a small printer badge. The details panel shows the picture, credits the designer, shows the licence (non-commercial and no-derivatives licences are called out), print status, filament and a **View on MakerWorld** button.
- **Parts to print.** The stats count printed parts and how many are still to print. **Export → Parts to print** lists them with picture, colour, status and link, and you can print it or save it as CSV or Markdown. The CSV bill of materials, PDF and printable view include each printed part's model link, designer and licence. JSON exports keep all of this, with the pictures included unless you choose *JSON without 3D-print thumbnails*.
- **3D printed** filter in the catalog, and the *My RackMate T1* example now holds its NVR Instant in a MakerWorld mount.
