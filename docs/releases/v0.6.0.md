## What's new

- **Power points for the wall.** Single and double AU/NZ power points (including one with USB-A and USB-C charging), plus UK, EU Schuko and US duplex outlets. Their sockets count towards the outlets your gear needs, so a plug-pack on the wall has somewhere to go.
- **Gear lies down when it doesn't fit standing up.** The nbn HFC NTD (Arris CM8200B) is too tall to stand on a shelf, so it now lies on its side automatically when you drag it, click it in the catalog, duplicate it, or move it between the wall and the rack. The same goes for any shelf gear that only fits one way. Press **R** to stand it back up.
- **Right-click to pan.** Hold the right mouse button and drag to move around the rack when you're zoomed in.
